This document is the newest authority for boss assignments while the complete world-by-world level plans are authored.

## Camp Head correction

- Camp Head is removed from World 6.
- Camp Head is not the final boss.
- The current final-boss identity is **General Gobbler**.
- Do not silently restore Camp Head under either assignment.
- Historical art, charts, prompts, manifests, and plans showing Camp Head in World 6 or as the final boss are superseded.

## World 6 history resolution

The latest retrieved approved World 6 change identifies **Avalanche Yeti** as the replacement boss after Camp Head was removed. No newer retrieved approval names another World 6 boss. Therefore Avalanche Yeti is the canonical World 6 boss until explicitly changed by the user.

## Boss implementation rules

- Every boss is unique to its assigned world.
- Every boss requires five earned damage events rather than a generic three-hit structure.
- Damage events should use changing environmental or mechanical solutions where appropriate instead of repeating one stomp five times.
- Boss approach courses must visually and mechanically foreshadow their assigned boss.
- World plans may expand arenas, phases, attacks, and set pieces, but must not replace or rename these bosses.

## World 2 shipwreck requirement

World 2 remains an aquatic/coastal setting with no underwater level or swimming course. The world is organized around a colossal shipwreck that grows from distant landmark to playable location across the world. The final course culminates inside the ship and leads directly into the Wraithbound boss fight.

## World-plan identity requirements

Every course plan must define:

- course landmark;
- relationship to the world's macro-landmark;
- primary traversal mechanic;
- signature set piece;
- start and end visual states;
- weather state and transition;
- environmental storytelling;
- persistent consequence affecting a later course;
- unique silhouette object;
- boss or future-course foreshadowing;
- multiplayer-safe shared access;
- one genuine hidden reward chest and one indistinguishable mimic;
- three Compass Coins;
- one authored hidden exit in the designated secret-exit course.

A course fails review if it lacks a memorable event, visually blends into another course, uses cosmetic-only weather, or could be transplanted into another world without major redesign.
