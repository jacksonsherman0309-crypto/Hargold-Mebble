# Late-Game Five-Course Structure Amendment

This document is the newest authority for Secret World A, Secret World B, and World 10 campaign structure.

## Locked structure

Worlds 8, 9, and 10 each contain exactly five mandatory courses total.

The five-course sequence is:

1. Course 1
2. Course 2
3. Course 3
4. Course 4
5. Boss course

The boss fight is included in the five-course total. These worlds do not contain five standard courses plus a separate boss course.

## Secret World A — World 8

- Exactly five mandatory courses total.
- Course 5 culminates in the Bone Crusher boss fight.
- No forked route.
- No alternate progression course.
- No hidden-exit level.
- No secret ninth course.
- Hidden collectibles, hidden reward chests, the world treasure trove, and optional in-course challenge rooms remain permitted.

## Secret World B — World 9

- Exactly five mandatory courses total.
- Course 5 culminates in the Tempest Warden boss fight.
- No forked route.
- No alternate progression course.
- No hidden-exit level.
- No secret ninth course.
- Hidden collectibles, hidden reward chests, the world treasure trove, and optional in-course challenge rooms remain permitted.

## Final World — World 10

- Exactly five mandatory courses total.
- Course 5 culminates in the General Gobbler final boss fight.
- No forked route.
- No alternate progression course.
- No hidden-exit level.
- No secret sixth course.
- All five courses are mandatory parts of the final campaign sequence.
- Hidden collectibles and optional in-course rewards may remain, but cannot open an additional course.

## Design purpose

Worlds 8 and 9 are concentrated mastery campaigns. World 10 is a concise final campaign. Their shortened structure must not be treated as permission to reuse generic layouts or reduce production quality.

Every course must still have:

- a unique landmark;
- a primary traversal identity;
- a memorable set piece;
- a visible start-to-finish transformation;
- authored enemy choreography;
- three Compass Coins where the global collectible rules apply;
- one genuine hidden reward chest and one indistinguishable chest mimic where completion-bearing-course rules apply;
- multiplayer-safe hero access;
- an authored weather or atmospheric state where appropriate.

## Supersession

This amendment supersedes any older document, schema, runtime value, level map, or prompt that assigns Worlds 8, 9, or 10:

- eight standard levels;
- a fork pair;
- a hidden-exit course;
- a secret ninth level;
- five standard courses plus a sixth boss course;
- any total other than exactly five courses including the boss fight.

Codex must update conflicting campaign totals, menus, world maps, progression checks, save data, completion percentages, unlock conditions, course manifests, and validation tests to this structure when those systems are implemented or migrated.
