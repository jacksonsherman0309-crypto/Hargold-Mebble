# Clean-room character construction benchmark

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This document records the permitted construction lessons from the user-supplied
platformer reference images. Those images were inspected in-session only and
are not copied into this repository. They do not replace the locked Hargold and
Mebble identity sheets, the approved compact/tall frame, or the canonical metre
scale.

## Permitted observations

The external references may inform:

- compact-versus-tall body-volume distribution;
- solid-black silhouette readability at gameplay distance;
- soft shoulder-to-arm, neck-to-chest, hip-to-leg, and ankle-to-foot flow;
- limb taper and rounded joint volume;
- where a deformation-ready mesh needs concentrated density;
- profile readability in neutral, run, jump, landing, and skid poses; and
- the general finish expected from a polished console platformer.

They may not supply facial features, clothing, colors, hats, logos,
accessories, textures, character identity, vertices, topology, rigs, weights,
animation data, or proprietary numerical measurements.

## Hargold construction target

Hargold remains the original locked short, broad explorer. His compact frame is
approximately `2.94` head heights tall because the approved normalized head
height is `0.34`. His construction must emphasize a large rounded head, short
integrated neck, broad deformable chest, round belly, thick tapered limbs,
large rounded boots, and a low stable center of gravity.

His profile must preserve clear negative space between hand, forearm, belly,
backpack, thigh, and boot. Thick does not mean cylindrical: the upper arm,
elbow, forearm, wrist, thigh, knee, calf, and ankle require intentional changes
in section and clean deformation loops.

Source reference board:
`assets/previews/construction-reference/hargold-construction-reference.png`.

## Mebble construction target

Mebble remains the original locked tall, thin explorer at `1.26` times
Hargold's height. His construction must emphasize the visibly tapered long
neck, protruding Adam's apple, longer torso and limbs, narrower shoulders,
waist and hips, slim tapered extremities, and a lighter higher center of
gravity.

The neck must flow continuously from chest to skull and stay visible in front
and profile. His arms and legs may be slender but may not become uniform tubes.
The cape, glasses, hands, feet, and neck must remain separate readable masses
in gameplay silhouette.

Source reference board:
`assets/previews/construction-reference/mebble-construction-reference.png`.

## Topology gate

The source-reference boards use the original approved featureless frames and
show neutral, run-contact, run-extension, jump-apex, landing, and solid
silhouette targets. They are the construction target.

Separate current-body audits expose the active connected body without costume
or identity detail and show neutral front, true side, wireframe, side
silhouette, and run deformation:

- `assets/previews/construction-reference/hargold-current-body-audit.png`;
- `assets/previews/construction-reference/mebble-current-body-audit.png`.

The audits are diagnostic evidence, not target art or proof of final topology.

The active organic-union bodies pass connected-component, watertightness,
binding, scale, and motion-range checks. Their dense automatic quad remesh is a
structural deformation candidate, not a substitute for senior character-artist
retopology. Final approval still requires purpose-built edge flow around the
shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, ankles, neck, mouth, and eyelids;
corrective shapes; hand and facial refinement; and every-pose inspection.

The July 28 directive locks the original Meshy models and authorizes body
animation refinement on those exact rigs. This historical construction
benchmark remains reference-only and cannot reactivate a replacement mesh.
