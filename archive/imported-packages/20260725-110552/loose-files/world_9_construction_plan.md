# World 9 — Chrono Archive Construction Plan (Build 022)

- **Campaign role:** Second secret/postgame world
- **Levels:** 9
- **Compass Star Coins:** 27
- **Hidden coins:** 9
- **Expert-hidden coins:** 5
- **Boss:** Tempest Warden
- **Guardian system:** Removed

## World identity

Chrono Archive is a clockwork library existing simultaneously in past, present and future states. Its difficulty comes from planning around deterministic recorded motion—not random time effects. Every replay, rewind, aging cycle and future preview has an exact visual or audio tell.

## World-map graph

Main path: 9-1 → 9-2 → 9-3 → 9-4 → 9-5 → 9-6 → 9-7 → 9-9

Secret path: 9-3 → 9-8 → 9-9

# 9-1 — Secondhand Steps

**Role:** Secret-world introduction  
**Target time:** 2:20-2:50  
**Difficulty:** 4.45/5  
**Identity:** This is the level where the player crosses platforms whose previous positions return as temporary solid echoes three seconds later.

**Primary mechanic:** Moving platforms continuously record and replay their recent positions as temporary echo platforms  
**Secondary mechanic:** Large clock hands give exact visual timing for every replay window  
**Checkpoint:** On a permanent brass balcony after the first complete three-platform replay chain, approximately 51% through the course.

## Seven-beat flow

1. **Establish:** One platform moves above solid ground while a translucent copy traces its position and becomes briefly solid three seconds later.
2. **Safe demonstration:** Two platforms create overlapping echoes above a wide recovery floor, teaching that the echo follows the platform rather than the player.
3. **First test:** The first archive gap requires riding the live platform, dropping onto its earlier echo and wall-jumping to the far shelf.
4. **Rule expansion:** Gear Bots add weight to moving platforms, changing where the recorded echo will appear without changing its delay.
5. **Midpoint mutation:** After the checkpoint, live platforms and their echoes move in opposite directions, creating crossing routes.
6. **Combination gauntlet:** Lantern Wisps conceal selected clock-hand tells while Spike Beetles occupy the permanent landing shelves.
7. **Final statement:** A staircase of six platform echoes assembles behind the moving originals and must be climbed before the oldest step expires.

## Enemy placement

- **Gear Bot ×6:** Changes recorded platform positions through added weight
- **Lantern Wisp ×5:** Temporarily conceals replay timing tells
- **Spike Beetle ×5:** Controls permanent landing shelves

## Power-up support

- Size-up before the first true echo gap
- Bubblebloom at the brass checkpoint balcony
- Stonefist behind a delayed-position block wall

## Compass Star Coins

- **9-1-C1 — visible_challenge:** Ride the first live platform past the visible coin, then collect it from the platform’s returning echo.
- **9-1-C2 — route_commitment:** Allow a Gear Bot to weigh down the center platform, use its lower recorded echo and enter the optional underside archive.
- **9-1-C3 — well_hidden:** Notice one echo stair appearing before its matching platform reaches that position, follow the early copy into the concealed secondhand chamber and exit through the back of the clock face.

## Character requirements

- **Mebble:** Mebble clears one grouped long transfer between opposing live and echo platforms after the checkpoint.
- **Hargold-only blocks:** 4 blocks in 1 group(s); uses: Delayed-position Stonefist cache, Return path from the secondhand chamber.
- **Switching:** The required Mebble obstacles are grouped into one contained section. Main-path completion does not require repeated back-and-forth switching; additional swaps are reserved for optional rooms, secret exits and Compass Star Coins.

## Difficulty modes

- **Easy:** Echo platforms remain solid longer and hidden clock-hand tells continue to glow through Wisp darkness.
- **Standard:** Uses the intended baseline timing, state persistence and enemy composition.
- **Hard:** Echo duration is shorter and one Gear Bot begins on the first required platform.
- **Nightmare:** The final staircase uses two replay delays at once, but the clock face displays both deterministic countdowns.

**Final signature:** The live staircase moves away while its recorded past assembles into the only route to the goal.

**Map connection:** normal → 9-2; secret → none

# 9-2 — Agebound Stacks

**Role:** Alternate interior course  
**Target time:** 2:25-2:55  
**Difficulty:** 4.55/5  
**Identity:** This is the level where the player moves library shelves and mechanisms between new, stable and decayed ages to create traversable versions of the same room.

**Primary mechanic:** Age switches cycle marked objects through new, stable and decayed physical states  
**Secondary mechanic:** Different materials age at different rates and retain their state when leaving the camera  
**Checkpoint:** Inside a stone preservation vault after the first three-room age cycle, approximately 54% through the course.

## Seven-beat flow

1. **Establish:** A wooden shelf cycles from intact to bowed to collapsed over solid ground while a stone shelf remains unchanged.
2. **Safe demonstration:** The player restores one broken bridge to its new state and ages a hanging chain until it reaches the floor.
3. **First test:** The first shaft requires creating a new wooden bridge, crossing it and then aging its counterweight to raise the exit platform.
4. **Rule expansion:** Crate Mimics age differently from real crates, exposing their identity before they attack.
5. **Midpoint mutation:** After the checkpoint, age switches affect only one material family at a time and require route planning.
6. **Combination gauntlet:** Gear Bots operate material selectors while Lantern Wisps temporarily obscure age-state markings.
7. **Final statement:** An entire archive aisle ages from new to ruin in a fixed wave, and each collapsing shelf becomes the next platform.

## Enemy placement

- **Crate Mimic ×5:** Age-state difference reveals disguised enemies
- **Gear Bot ×6:** Operates material-specific age selectors
- **Lantern Wisp ×4:** Obscures state markings

## Power-up support

- Stonefist before the first collapsed stone seal
- Size-up in the preservation vault
- Ice Power on the optional metal-stack route

## Compass Star Coins

- **9-2-C1 — visible_challenge:** Restore the first upper shelf, cross it and collect the visible coin before it bows into its stable state.
- **9-2-C2 — route_commitment:** Age the hanging chain to floor level, carry its attached weight to the side selector and restore the optional archive bridge.
- **9-2-C3 — well_hidden:** Observe that one bookcase gathers dust without losing color, restore it twice beyond the normal new state and enter the preserved room behind it.

## Character requirements

- **Mebble:** Mebble clears one grouped high transfer across shelves that remain intact only in the new state.
- **Hargold-only blocks:** 6 blocks in 2 group(s); uses: Preserved rear archive, Material-selector shortcut.
- **Switching:** The required Mebble obstacles are grouped into one contained section. Main-path completion does not require repeated back-and-forth switching; additional swaps are reserved for optional rooms, secret exits and Compass Star Coins.

## Difficulty modes

- **Easy:** Age-state transitions are slower and each object displays its next state before changing.
- **Standard:** Uses the intended baseline timing, state persistence and enemy composition.
- **Hard:** Material selectors reset sooner and Mimics wake immediately when restored.
- **Nightmare:** The final aisle contains two aging waves moving in opposite directions, with every object’s timeline visibly marked.

**Final signature:** A full archive aisle collapses in sequence and turns its own ruins into a descending path.

**Map connection:** normal → 9-3; secret → none

# 9-3 — Pendulum Paradox

**Target time:** 2:25-2:55  
**Difficulty:** 4.65/5  
**Identity:** This is the level where the player swings from giant pendulums while past and future copies show where each pendulum was and where it will be.

**Secondary mechanic:** Time anchors can swap which copy is physically active  
**Checkpoint:** On a fixed clocktower landing after the first three-pendulum transfer, approximately 51% through the course.

## Seven-beat flow

1. **Establish:** A low pendulum swings above solid ground with a blue past trail and a gold future preview.
2. **Safe demonstration:** One time anchor swaps the solid state from the live pendulum to its past copy over a recovery floor.
3. **First test:** The first tower gap requires releasing from the live pendulum, landing on its solid past copy and jumping to the next.
4. **Rule expansion:** Shellbacks can ride pendulums and activate anchors when they pass remote switches.
5. **Midpoint mutation:** After the checkpoint, future copies briefly become solid while the live pendulum enters stasis.
6. **Combination gauntlet:** Shock Jellies occupy the apexes while Lantern Wisps hide selected future previews.
7. **Final statement:** Five pendulums swing through one another in different timeline states and form a single uninterrupted aerial route.

## Enemy placement

- **Shellback ×3:** Remote time-anchor activator
- **Shock Jelly ×5:** Controls pendulum apexes
- **Lantern Wisp ×5:** Hides future previews

## Power-up support

- Bubblebloom before the first remote anchor
- Size-up at the clocktower checkpoint
- Stonefist behind the lower pendulum support

## Compass Star Coins

- **9-3-C1 — visible_challenge:** Release at the first pendulum’s apex and collect the visible coin from its solid past copy.
- **9-3-C2 — route_commitment:** Kick a Shellback onto the lower pendulum, let it trigger the remote anchor and enter the future-copy side tower.
- **9-3-C3 — expert_hidden:** Preserve the opening anchor instead of using it, recognize the repeated three-chime phrase after the checkpoint and activate past, live and future pendulums in that order to open the secret-exit clock face.

## Character requirements

- **Mebble:** Two Mebble-required extended releases are grouped into the future-solid section after the checkpoint.
- **Hargold-only blocks:** 5 blocks in 2 group(s); uses: Lower support cache, Secret-exit anchor mechanism.
- **Switching:** The required Mebble obstacles are grouped into one contained section. Main-path completion does not require repeated back-and-forth switching; additional swaps are reserved for optional rooms, secret exits and Compass Star Coins.

## Difficulty modes

- **Easy:** Past copies remain solid longer and future previews are brighter.
- **Standard:** Uses the intended baseline timing, state persistence and enemy composition.
- **Hard:** Anchor windows are shorter and Shock Jellies occupy one additional transfer apex.
- **Nightmare:** The five-pendulum finale swaps active timelines twice during each swing, but the chimes always announce the exact deterministic order.

**Final signature:** Past, present and future pendulums overlap into one aerial path that exists for only a single complete swing cycle.

**Map connection:** normal → 9-4; secret → 9-8

# 9-4 — Rewind Reservoir

**Role:** Stronghold set piece without guardian  
**Target time:** 2:30-3:00  
**Difficulty:** 4.7/5  
**Identity:** This is the level where the player crosses a clockwork reservoir whose water, gates and machinery periodically rewind while the player and anchored objects continue forward.

**Primary mechanic:** Environmental rewind returns unanchored objects to their positions from four seconds earlier  
**Secondary mechanic:** Time anchors preserve selected objects and switches through each rewind  
**Checkpoint:** On a permanent anchor dais after the first full reservoir rewind, approximately 55% through the course.

## Seven-beat flow

1. **Establish:** A harmless water wheel turns forward and then visibly retraces its motion while the player remains unaffected.
2. **Safe demonstration:** Anchoring a floating platform prevents it from rewinding and creates a permanent step over a shallow channel.
3. **First test:** The first fatal reservoir requires riding a gate forward, anchoring it and surviving the water’s reverse current.
4. **Rule expansion:** Tidebiters rewind along their recorded swim path and can be predicted or frozen before the reset.
5. **Midpoint mutation:** After the checkpoint, only half the room rewinds at a time, creating alternating present and past zones.
6. **Combination gauntlet:** Camp Sentry projectiles replay backward through gates while Gear Bots operate the anchor selectors.
7. **Final statement:** The player overloads the reservoir clock, causing gates, water and platforms to rewind in staggered waves during the escape.

## Enemy placement

- **Tidebiter ×5:** Replays predictable swim paths through reverse currents
- **Camp Sentry ×5:** Projectiles retrace paths during rewind
- **Gear Bot ×6:** Operates anchor selectors

## Power-up support

- Ice Power before the first Tidebiter rewind
- Stonefist at the anchor-dais checkpoint
- Bubblebloom in the optional half-rewind route

## Compass Star Coins

- **9-4-C1 — visible_challenge:** Anchor the first floating platform at its highest point and collect the visible coin when the surrounding reservoir rewinds.
- **9-4-C2 — route_commitment:** Freeze a Tidebiter at the far current switch so it remains in place while the water and gate return to their prior positions.
- **9-4-C3 — expert_hidden:** Anchor the opening maintenance gate rather than the reward platform, preserve that choice through the checkpoint and enter the cistern exposed only when the opposite half of the reservoir rewinds.

## Character requirements

- **Mebble:** Mebble clears one grouped high transfer between the two alternating rewind zones.
- **Hargold-only blocks:** 7 blocks in 2 group(s); uses: Expert maintenance cistern, Anchor-overload recovery cache.
- **Switching:** The required Mebble obstacles are grouped into one contained section. Main-path completion does not require repeated back-and-forth switching; additional swaps are reserved for optional rooms, secret exits and Compass Star Coins.

## Difficulty modes

- **Easy:** Rewind warnings are longer and reverse currents are weaker.
- **Standard:** Uses the intended baseline timing, state persistence and enemy composition.
- **Hard:** Only two objects can remain anchored simultaneously and Sentry shots retrace faster.
- **Nightmare:** The escape uses three staggered rewind waves whose order is determined by the anchors retained earlier, with every wave shown on the master clock.

**Final signature:** The entire stronghold physically retraces its collapse while the player continues moving forward through it.

**Map connection:** normal → 9-5; secret → none
