# World 10 — Ironwood Siege Construction Plan (Build 023)

> **Name status:** Ironwood Siege is the current working title. The complete world plan is not dependent on retaining this exact name.

- **Campaign role:** 100%-completion final world
- **Levels:** 9
- **Compass Star Coins:** 27
- **Hidden coins:** 9
- **Expert-hidden coins:** 7
- **Boss:** General Gobbler
- **Guardian system:** Removed

## World identity

Ironwood Siege combines a dense wilderness, hunting traps, rope bridges, timber machinery, command stockades, moving war vehicles and General Gobbler’s final command citadel. The playable route remains a strict side-scrolling plane. The world is substantially harder than every earlier world but keeps all traps, commands and machinery deterministic and visibly telegraphed.

# 10-1 — Timberline Breach

**Role:** Final-world introduction  
**Target time:** 2:25-2:55  
**Difficulty:** 4.75/5  
**Identity:** This is the level where the player breaches the outer Ironwood war line while command banners visibly change enemy formations, bridge access and artillery timing.

**Primary mechanic:** Command banners switch nearby patrols and machinery among advance, hold and retreat states  
**Secondary mechanic:** Breakable barricades create alternate routes but also release stored logs and enemies  
**Checkpoint:** Inside a captured watch platform after the first complete three-banner command cycle, approximately 52% through the course.

## Seven-beat flow

1. **Establish:** A red advance banner moves Camp Critters across a harmless training yard while a blue hold banner stops them on marked positions.
2. **Safe demonstration:** The player changes one banner over solid ground and watches a rope bridge extend when the patrol reaches its hold marker.
3. **First test:** The first ravine requires setting a patrol to advance, passing beneath it and switching to hold so its weight lowers the bridge.
4. **Rule expansion:** Camp Sentry firing lanes rotate with banner state and can be redirected into barricade locks.
5. **Midpoint mutation:** After the checkpoint, banners control two separated groups at once, making one route safer while another becomes active.
6. **Combination gauntlet:** Camp Chippers remove cover, Shellbacks change bridge weight and Sentries fire through opening barricades.
7. **Final statement:** Three command banners must be changed during one continuous push while the outer stockade collapses behind the player.

## Enemy placement

- **Camp Critter ×7:** Demonstrates command-state patrol movement
- **Camp Sentry ×5:** Banner-controlled projectile lanes
- **Camp Chipper ×4:** Removes wooden cover and remains a one-hit enemy
- **Shellback ×3:** Movable bridge counterweight

## Power-up support

- Size-up before the first ravine
- Stonefist at the captured watch checkpoint
- Bubblebloom behind the second barricade

## Compass Star Coins

- **10-1-C1 — visible_challenge:** Set the opening patrol to hold on the lower bridge plate and collect the visible coin from the raised bridge end.
- **10-1-C2 — route_commitment:** Reflect the first Sentry shot through the retreat-state firing lane and enter the optional watchtower route.
- **10-1-C3 — well_hidden:** Notice one command pennant pointing against the wind, set the three banners to retreat-hold-advance and enter the maintenance trench exposed behind the outer stockade.

## Character requirements

- **Mebble:** Mebble clears one grouped long transfer between the upper and lower command bridges after the checkpoint.
- **Hargold-only blocks:** 5 blocks in 2 group(s); uses: Maintenance trench reward, Barricade bypass and return route.
- **Switching:** The required Mebble obstacles are grouped into one contained section. Main-path completion does not require repeated back-and-forth switching; additional swaps are reserved for optional rooms, the secret exit and Compass Star Coins.

## Difficulty modes

- **Easy:** Banner states remain active longer and patrol markers glow continuously.
- **Standard:** Uses the intended baseline timings, patrol composition and machinery state persistence.
- **Hard:** The two-group banner section has shorter state windows and one additional Sentry lane.
- **Nightmare:** All three banner groups remain active during the final collapse, but every state transition retains a fixed horn cue and visible route marker.

**Final signature:** The player advances through the war line as banners, patrols and bridges change state in one synchronized push.

**Map connection:** normal → 10-2; secret → none

# 10-2 — Snarewood Run

**Role:** Wilderness trap course  
**Target time:** 2:25-2:55  
**Difficulty:** 4.8/5  
**Identity:** This is the level where the player crosses a dense hunting forest filled with clearly telegraphed snares, counterweighted logs and false camp supplies.

**Primary mechanic:** Visible trip lines trigger pendulum logs, net lifts and ground snares  
**Secondary mechanic:** Cutting or weighing one trap changes the armed state of connected traps  
**Checkpoint:** On a broad hunter platform after the first linked snare network, approximately 50% through the course.

## Seven-beat flow

1. **Establish:** One bright trip line releases a small log over solid ground and resets slowly enough to show the complete mechanism.
2. **Safe demonstration:** A net snare lifts the player to an optional branch and can be escaped with a jump or ground slam.
3. **First test:** The first fatal gap requires intentionally triggering a pendulum log and using it as a temporary crossing.
4. **Rule expansion:** Crate Mimics replace some supply crates while real crates can hold trip lines down.
5. **Midpoint mutation:** After the checkpoint, connected traps share counterweights, so disabling one arms another.
6. **Combination gauntlet:** Acorn Bombers strike trip lines from above while Spike Beetles occupy the safe roots between mechanisms.
7. **Final statement:** A descending forest run chains four traps into a single route: log swing, net lift, rolling trunk and collapsing blind.

## Enemy placement

- **Crate Mimic ×5:** Disguises itself as trap-control weight
- **Acorn Bomber ×5:** Accidentally or intentionally triggers trip lines
- **Spike Beetle ×5:** Controls safe root platforms
- **Camp Critter ×5:** Adds readable patrol pressure

## Power-up support

- Bubblebloom before the first linked traps
- Size-up at the hunter-platform checkpoint
- Stonefist beneath the heavy counterweight nest

## Compass Star Coins

- **10-2-C1 — visible_challenge:** Trigger the first pendulum log late, ride its outward swing and jump through the visible coin above the far root.
- **10-2-C2 — route_commitment:** Carry the real supply crate past the Mimic nest and use it to hold the optional net-lift counterweight.
- **10-2-C3 — well_hidden:** Follow a trip line that disappears into an apparently solid tree, ground-slam the hollow root beneath its counterweight and enter the concealed trapper tunnel.

## Character requirements

- **Mebble:** Mebble clears one grouped canopy transfer between two net-lift release points after the checkpoint.
- **Hargold-only blocks:** 6 blocks in 2 group(s); uses: Heavy counterweight nest, Concealed trapper tunnel.
- **Switching:** The required Mebble obstacles are grouped into one contained section. Main-path completion does not require repeated back-and-forth switching; additional swaps are reserved for optional rooms, the secret exit and Compass Star Coins.

## Difficulty modes

- **Easy:** Trip lines glow longer and trap reset timing is slower.
- **Standard:** Uses the intended baseline timings, patrol composition and machinery state persistence.
- **Hard:** Connected counterweights transfer more quickly and Acorn Bombers target active lines more often.
- **Nightmare:** The four-trap finale preserves every earlier counterweight state and requires the player to use the resulting deterministic sequence rather than a fixed route.

**Final signature:** Four hunting traps trigger one another and turn the forest itself into a moving obstacle course.

**Map connection:** normal → 10-3; secret → none

5. **Midpoint mutation:** After the checkpoint, two opposing cannons share one rotating platform and alternate firing ownership.
6. **Combination gauntlet:** Flutterbugs occupy aerial transfer points while Camp Sentries cover the fixed tree crowns.
7. **Final statement:** Three cannons fire in sequence and their impacts tilt one enormous rope bridge into a rising launch ramp.

## Enemy placement

- **Bomb Bird ×6:** Changes bridge weight and damages cannon shields
- **Flutterbug ×5:** Aerial transfer and bounce timing
- **Camp Sentry ×5:** Controls permanent tree crowns
- **Shellback ×3:** Movable bridge tension weight

## Compass Star Coins

- **10-3-C3 — expert_hidden:** Read the three feather marks carved into separate cannon wheels, fire them in low-high-middle order and ride the resulting reverse bridge pitch into the hidden treetop arsenal.

## Character requirements

## Difficulty modes

- **Easy:** Cannon aiming guides remain visible and rope bridges settle more slowly.
- **Standard:** Uses the intended baseline timings, patrol composition and machinery state persistence.
- **Hard:** Opposing cannons alternate faster and one additional Bomb Bird joins the final bridge.
- **Nightmare:** The final three-cannon sequence changes bridge tension based on retained weights, but each valid impact order remains visibly marked on the cannon wheels.

# 10-4 — Stockade Sabotage

**Role:** Stronghold infiltration without guardian  
**Target time:** 2:35-3:05  
**Difficulty:** 4.9/5  
**Identity:** This is the level where the player infiltrates a layered wooden stockade and disables its alarm, gate and counterweight networks before the entire structure folds inward.

**Primary mechanic:** Alarm cords activate patrol doors, Sentry lanes and locking barricades in linked zones  
**Secondary mechanic:** Gate counterweights can be cut, carried or frozen to preserve specific route states  
**Checkpoint:** Inside the central signal room after the first complete alarm-network shutdown, approximately 55% through the course.

## Seven-beat flow

1. **Establish:** One alarm cord closes a harmless gate and draws a visible red path to the Sentry it activates.
2. **Safe demonstration:** The player cuts a cord, watches its counterweight fall and learns that the gate remains open until the weight resets.
3. **First test:** The first stockade chamber requires passing one alarm line, then using its activated Sentry shot to break the next lock.
4. **Rule expansion:** Gear Bots repair cut cords unless diverted onto false maintenance routes.
5. **Midpoint mutation:** After the checkpoint, alarm zones overlap and disabling one transfers its patrol to the neighboring zone.
6. **Combination gauntlet:** Camp Chippers remove temporary cover while Shellbacks hold counterweights and Sentry shots cross multiple gate openings.
7. **Final statement:** Overloading the signal drum causes the stockade walls to hinge inward and become temporary platforms during the escape.

## Enemy placement

- **Gear Bot ×6:** Repairs alarm cords and operates maintenance routes
- **Camp Sentry ×6:** Alarm-linked projectile pressure
- **Camp Chipper ×5:** Removes cover and remains one-hit
- **Shellback ×3:** Movable gate counterweight

## Power-up support

- Stonefist before the first reinforced signal wall
- Ice Power in the central signal room
- Size-up before the collapsing escape

## Compass Star Coins

- **10-4-C1 — visible_challenge:** Use the opening alarm’s Sentry shot to break the upper gate lock and collect the visible coin from the lifted gate beam.
- **10-4-C2 — route_commitment:** Freeze the second gate weight at maximum height and enter the optional maintenance loft before Gear Bots repair the cord.
- **10-4-C3 — expert_hidden:** Leave the first alarm active, route its patrol through two empty zones and strike the unguarded rear signal drum to expose the command archive under the stockade floor.

## Character requirements

- **Mebble:** Mebble clears one grouped high transfer between overlapping alarm balconies after the checkpoint.
- **Hargold-only blocks:** 7 blocks in 2 group(s); uses: Rear command archive, Reinforced signal-wall shortcut.
- **Switching:** The required Mebble obstacles are grouped into one contained section. Main-path completion does not require repeated back-and-forth switching; additional swaps are reserved for optional rooms, the secret exit and Compass Star Coins.

## Difficulty modes

- **Easy:** Alarm transfers are slower and cut cords take longer to repair.
- **Standard:** Uses the intended baseline timings, patrol composition and machinery state persistence.
- **Hard:** Gear Bots repair more quickly and overlapping alarm zones share one additional Sentry.
- **Nightmare:** The escape preserves every remaining alarm state, causing different walls to fold in a deterministic order displayed by the central signal map.

**Final signature:** The stockade destroys itself as its walls hinge into the player’s escape path.

**Map connection:** normal → 10-5; secret → none

# 10-5 — Chipperworks

**Role:** Industrial forest course  
**Target time:** 2:35-3:05  
**Difficulty:** 4.95/5  
**Identity:** This is the level where the player crosses a war-camp timber mill whose logs, saw carriers and chip hoppers can be redirected into machinery or used as moving terrain.

**Primary mechanic:** Log conveyors route heavy timber through saw carriers, lifts and chip hoppers  
**Secondary mechanic:** Mechanical diverters preserve their state and change multiple downstream machines  
**Checkpoint:** On a reinforced mill platform after the first full log-routing circuit, approximately 51% through the course.

## Seven-beat flow

1. **Establish:** A slow log conveyor crosses solid ground while a Camp Chipper visibly feeds harmless branches into a side hopper.
2. **Safe demonstration:** One diverter changes a log from a saw lane to a lift lane and creates a broad moving platform.
3. **First test:** The first fatal machinery gap requires riding a routed log beneath a saw carrier and jumping before it enters the hopper.
4. **Rule expansion:** Camp Chippers change diverter positions when they reach control handles, but each remains a one-hit enemy.
5. **Midpoint mutation:** After the checkpoint, cut logs become shorter faster platforms while uncut logs can jam heavy presses.
6. **Combination gauntlet:** Rock Runts add weight to conveyor lifts and Boom Barrels can destroy or prematurely split logs.
7. **Final statement:** The player jams the main chipper with one reinforced log and rides the cascading machinery failure out of the mill.

## Enemy placement

- **Camp Chipper ×7:** Core mill worker; changes diverters and dies in one hit
- **Rock Runt ×4:** Heavy conveyor weight; avoidable or contained in one Hargold section
- **Boom Barrel ×4:** Splits logs and overloads machinery
- **Gear Bot ×5:** Operates conveyor selectors

## Power-up support

- Stonefist before the reinforced-log chamber
- Ice Power at the mill checkpoint
- Bubblebloom on the optional upper conveyor

## Compass Star Coins

- **10-5-C1 — visible_challenge:** Route the opening log onto the lift lane and collect the visible coin while riding it above the saw carrier.
- **10-5-C2 — route_commitment:** Preserve one uncut log through the first diverter and use it to jam the side press leading to the optional loft.
- **10-5-C3 — expert_hidden:** Notice sawdust falling from a stopped hopper with no visible input, reverse the three upstream diverters and enter the hidden service chute behind the jammed machine.

## Character requirements

- **Mebble:** Mebble clears one grouped transfer between separated upper conveyor lanes after the checkpoint.
- **Hargold-only blocks:** 6 blocks in 2 group(s); uses: Reinforced-log Hargold section, Hidden hopper service chute.
- **Switching:** The required Mebble obstacles are grouped into one contained section. Main-path completion does not require repeated back-and-forth switching; additional swaps are reserved for optional rooms, the secret exit and Compass Star Coins.

## Difficulty modes

- **Easy:** Conveyors move more slowly and saw carriers pause longer at each endpoint.
- **Standard:** Uses the intended baseline timings, patrol composition and machinery state persistence.
- **Hard:** Diverters reset sooner and Boom Barrels appear on one additional conveyor.
- **Nightmare:** The final machinery failure retains every log state and diverter choice, but warning lamps display the exact deterministic collapse order.

**Final signature:** A reinforced log stops the central chipper and the entire mill becomes a collapsing moving route.

## Difficulty modes

- **Easy:** Command tells remain visible longer, Dreadnought acceleration is reduced and one recovery heart is available before the summit phase.
- **Standard:** Uses the intended baseline timings, patrol composition and machinery state persistence.
- **Hard:** Command attacks chain sooner, the moving deck remains active during every volley and Bassquake alternates floor and air shockwaves more quickly.
- **Nightmare:** Chronus Rewind replays two prior arena states, False Surrender can occur after event three or four and the final charge has fewer anchor points.

**Final signature:** The command citadel, moving Dreadnought and ruined summit collapse into one final playable escape as General Gobbler is defeated exactly once.

**Map connection:** normal → none; secret → none

## Boss structure

**Boss:** General Gobbler — 5 earned damage events

- Command rotunda: damage events 1-2
- Ironwood Dreadnought battle: damage events 3-4
- Ruined summit and stolen Chronus engine: damage event 5
- Playable final escape and definitive ending
