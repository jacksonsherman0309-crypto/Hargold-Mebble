# World 1 — Verdant Vale: Construction-Ready Level Plan

**Scope:** nine complete levels, 27 Compass Star Coins, nine hidden coins, one expert-hidden coin. No UI or final art is included.

## World progression

- Main path: 1-1 → 1-2 → 1-3 → 1-4 → 1-5 → 1-6 → 1-7 → 1-9.
- The secret exit in 1-3 opens optional level 1-8, which rejoins before 1-9.
- 1-4 is the midworld guardian stage. 1-9 is the five-hit world boss stage.
- Each level contains exactly one hidden Compass Star Coin. The only expert-hidden coin in World 1 is 1-8-C3.

# 1-1 — Meadow Wake

**Role:** Campaign opener  
**Target time:** 1:55-2:20  
**Difficulty:** 1.0/5  
**Identity:** Learn the game by crossing a bright meadow where every terrain shape quietly teaches a movement rule.

**Primary mechanic:** Basic locomotion, jump arcs, slopes and readable coin guidance  
**Supporting mechanic:** Camp Critter and Shellback fundamentals  
**Checkpoint:** After the rope-bridge crossing, approximately 56% through the course.

## Seven-beat course flow

### 1. Establish
A broad safe meadow gives room to walk, run, stop and swap heroes. Low coin arcs establish the normal jump shape.

### 2. Safe demonstration
A single short gap, a low step and a downhill slope teach speed preservation with no lethal consequence beneath them.

### 3. First test
Camp Critters patrol separated platforms. The first one is isolated so side contact and stomp timing are readable.

### 4. Rule expansion
A Shellback beside breakable blocks demonstrates retracting, kicking and shell-driven destruction.

### 5. Midpoint mutation
After the checkpoint, terrain alternates between upper ledges and a lower creek path. Both remain visible so route choice feels intentional.

### 6. Combination gauntlet
A rope bridge, Spike Beetles and one Camp Sentry require moving between high and low cover while maintaining forward rhythm.

### 7. Final statement
A long rolling hill leads into three graduated gaps and a clean goal approach, combining speed, jump control and enemy reading.

## Enemy placement purpose

- **Camp Critter ×6:** Safe stomp and spacing lessons
- **Shellback ×3:** Introduces shell states and block breaking
- **Spike Beetle ×3:** Teaches avoidance rather than automatic stomping
- **Camp Sentry ×1:** Previews visible projectile timing

## Power-up support

- Size-up block before first enemy test
- Second block before checkpoint
- Optional Fire or Ice block on upper route

## Compass Star Coins

- **1-1-C1 — Visible mechanic challenge:** Follow the upper coin arc and jump from the springy fallen log before it settles.
- **1-1-C2 — Optional route commitment:** Kick the first Shellback through a short breakable column, then follow the opened upper route.
- **1-1-C3 — Well hidden:** Notice an interrupted low coin trail beside the brambles, drop behind the foreground grass and follow the concealed creek shelf.

## Difficulty changes

- **Easy:** Adds recovery ledges below the final gaps and removes one Spike Beetle.
- **Standard:** Uses the intended layout.
- **Hard:** Adds a second Sentry lane after the checkpoint and shortens the rope-bridge recovery space.
- **Nightmare:** Changes the final gaps into alternating falling platforms and makes the Shellback route more dangerous without changing the clues.

**Final signature moment:** A downhill run into a three-gap meadow panorama with the goal visible in the distance.

**Map result:** normal exit → 1-2

# 1-2 — Acorn Run

**Role:** Momentum course  
**Target time:** 2:00-2:30  
**Difficulty:** 1.3/5  
**Identity:** Build and preserve speed across rolling hills while falling acorns repeatedly alter the safest jump timing.

**Primary mechanic:** Momentum over slopes  
**Supporting mechanic:** Acorn Bomber drop timing and low ceilings  
**Checkpoint:** Inside the hollow oak after the first sustained momentum chain, approximately 50%.

## Seven-beat course flow

### 1. Establish
Two broad hills show that downhill running increases practical jump reach while uphill travel slows the player.

### 2. Safe demonstration
An Acorn Bomber drops acorns onto flat ground, clearly showing its shadow and release timing.

### 3. First test
Bombers patrol over a gap sequence where acorns roll downhill after landing.

### 4. Rule expansion
Low branches force the player to release jump early or crouch-slide beneath them instead of holding every jump.

### 5. Midpoint mutation
Inside the hollow oak, slopes become enclosed root ramps and acorns ricochet from wooden walls.

### 6. Combination gauntlet
A Shellback can clear rolling acorns, but careless kicks send the shell back down the same slopes.

### 7. Final statement
A sustained downhill run crosses alternating upper and lower platforms before a final leap through a falling-acorn curtain.

## Enemy placement purpose

- **Acorn Bomber ×7:** Creates predictable moving hazards tied to slope direction
- **Shellback ×2:** Optional momentum tool and rebound threat
- **Camp Critter ×4:** Ground pressure on hill crests

## Power-up support

- Size-up near start
- Ice Power in hollow-oak midpoint room
- Optional reserve block above final hill

## Compass Star Coins

- **1-2-C1 — Visible mechanic challenge:** Carry running speed from the first long descent and clear the high suspended ring.
- **1-2-C2 — Optional route commitment:** Preserve a Shellback through the hollow oak and use it to clear the lower block tunnel.
- **1-2-C3 — Well hidden:** Follow a downward coin arrow into an apparently dangerous gap; a hidden root shelf catches the player and leads behind the hill.

## Difficulty changes

- **Easy:** Acorn shadows appear earlier and the hidden shelf is wider.
- **Standard:** Uses the intended layout.
- **Hard:** Bombers release in alternating pairs and one low branch removes a safe stopping point.
- **Nightmare:** Acorns can trigger collapsing root platforms, changing the route timing rather than merely increasing speed.

**Final signature moment:** A full-speed descent under a moving canopy of falling acorns.

**Map result:** normal exit → 1-3

# 1-3 — Burrowbank

**Role:** Underground transformation and secret-exit course  
**Target time:** 2:15-2:45  
**Difficulty:** 1.6/5  
**Identity:** Descend through living root tunnels where soft dirt walls, burrowing enemies and moving roots conceal a second way out.

**Primary mechanic:** Soft-dirt passages and root lifts  
**Supporting mechanic:** Dirt Squirts and hidden-route clues  
**Checkpoint:** At the crystal-root basin before the vertical return shaft, approximately 54%.

## Seven-beat course flow

### 1. Establish
The bank entrance visibly crumbles when struck, establishing the visual language of soft dirt.

### 2. Safe demonstration
A Dirt Squirt travels beneath a transparent dirt strip before emerging onto safe ground.

### 3. First test
Root lifts move slowly over shallow pits while Dirt Squirts force the player to change platforms.

### 4. Rule expansion
Some soft walls reform after several seconds, creating temporary shortcuts but never trapping the player.

### 5. Midpoint mutation
The crystal-root basin adds reflected silhouettes that reveal burrowing enemies before they emerge.

### 6. Combination gauntlet
The return shaft combines vertical root lifts, Spike Beetles and reforming dirt ledges.

### 7. Final statement
Two exits become visible near the surface: the obvious bank opening and a sealed root door opened by a clue sequence found earlier.

## Enemy placement purpose

- **Dirt Squirt ×8:** Telegraphs movement through dirt and pressures lift transfers
- **Spike Beetle ×4:** Controls narrow root ledges
- **Camp Critter ×3:** Maintains familiar ground rhythm

## Power-up support

- Fire Power before soft-wall tutorial
- Size-up after checkpoint
- Optional Stonefist trial block near secret-exit mechanism

## Compass Star Coins

- **1-3-C1 — Visible mechanic challenge:** Ride the first root lift above its normal transfer point and jump into the visible side pocket.
- **1-3-C2 — Optional route commitment:** Break a temporary wall, enter before it reforms and outrun the closing dirt corridor.
- **1-3-C3 — Well hidden:** Follow the only coin reflection that points away from its real coin; strike the corresponding wall to reveal a hidden chamber.

## Secret exit

Three engraved root symbols appear in separate rooms. Striking the matching soft-wall sections in order keeps the final root door open and reveals the path to optional level 1-8. Each symbol is visible on the normal route, so the solution is observational rather than random.

## Difficulty changes

- **Easy:** Temporary dirt remains open longer and return-shaft pits gain recovery shelves.
- **Standard:** Uses the intended layout.
- **Hard:** Reforming walls close faster and Dirt Squirts overlap one lift transfer.
- **Nightmare:** Root lifts change direction after enemy impacts, producing a different but deterministic shaft puzzle.

**Final signature moment:** A split surface breakthrough where the hidden root door opens beside the ordinary exit.

**Map result:** normal exit → 1-4; secret exit → 1-8

# 1-4 — Ruin Rise

**Role:** Midworld tower and guardian  
**Target time:** 2:35-3:10  
**Difficulty:** 2.0/5  
**Identity:** Climb a broken woodland watchtower by rotating stairs and opening reinforced masonry before fighting a mechanical sentry guardian.

**Primary mechanic:** Rotating stair modules and vertical lifts  
**Supporting mechanic:** Reinforced blocks and Sentry fire  
**Checkpoint:** At the tower belfry immediately before the guardian approach, approximately 68%.

## Seven-beat course flow

### 1. Establish
The first stair module rotates over solid flooring, teaching its timing without fall risk.

### 2. Safe demonstration
Hargold breaks a low reinforced block while Mebble reaches an alternate overhead switch; both methods rotate the same stairs.

### 3. First test
Rotating stairs cross a narrow shaft with ledges beneath the first failed jumps.

### 4. Rule expansion
Camp Sentry projectiles can strike tower switches, letting the player use enemy fire to change stair orientation.

### 5. Midpoint mutation
The upper tower loses its outer wall, adding wind and moving hanging platforms while preserving the strict side plane.

### 6. Combination gauntlet
Three stair modules rotate on offset cycles while Sentry fire passes through shield openings.

### 7. Final statement
A rapid lift carries the player through the bell structure into the guardian arena.

## Enemy placement purpose

- **Camp Sentry ×5:** Projectile timing and remote switch activation
- **Shellback ×2:** Breaks low masonry and creates moving pressure
- **Spike Beetle ×3:** Guards narrow stair landings

## Power-up support

- Size-up before tower entrance
- Stonefist Gloves in visible pre-checkpoint block
- Recovery power-up before guardian door

## Compass Star Coins

- **1-4-C1 — Visible mechanic challenge:** Stay on the first rotating stair through a full turn and leap to its upper face.
- **1-4-C2 — Optional route commitment:** Redirect a Sentry shot into a side switch, rotate the stair away from the main route and ride it into the exterior alcove.
- **1-4-C3 — Well hidden:** Strike the bell with a rolling Shellback; the sound loosens a cracked interior panel that was shown earlier.

## Guardian — Camp Sentry Prime

**Required damage events:** 5  
**Arena:** Circular watchtower platform with two rotating shield walls and three projectile reflectors.

### Damage progression

- Hit 1 teaches reflecting one charged shot into its rear battery.
- Hit 2 adds sweeping low shots and one moving reflector.
- Hit 3 rotates the shield walls, changing safe cover.
- Hit 4 requires opening both shield walls with separate shots before the battery is exposed.
- Hit 5 combines a rapid three-shot pattern with collapsing edge plates; the final reflected shot overloads the core.

### Attack set

- Single aimed shot
- Low sweeping burst
- High arcing charge
- Reflector-disabling pulse
- Shield-wall ram
- Emergency rotating volley

## Difficulty changes

- **Easy:** Stairs pause at cardinal angles and the guardian uses slower single shots.
- **Standard:** Uses the intended layout.
- **Hard:** Stair pauses are removed and the guardian chains low and high shots.
- **Nightmare:** One shield wall remains damaged between phases, changing reflector access and safe cover.

**Final signature moment:** The tower bell breaks loose after the guardian defeat and forms a descending escape platform.

**Map result:** normal exit → 1-5

# 1-5 — Sentry Span

**Role:** Projectile-mechanic course  
**Target time:** 2:05-2:35  
**Difficulty:** 2.1/5  
**Identity:** Cross a layered bridge network where visible Sentry shots destroy cover, activate mechanisms and reshape the route.

**Primary mechanic:** Readable projectile lanes and destructible cover  
**Supporting mechanic:** Moving bridge sections  
**Checkpoint:** On the central stone pier after the first bridge collapse, approximately 51%.

## Seven-beat course flow

### 1. Establish
A Sentry fires across a wide bridge with multiple cover blocks and no pit beneath the lane.

### 2. Safe demonstration
Its shot breaks a brittle wall and reveals a normal-coin cache, establishing projectiles as tools.

### 3. First test
Two staggered lanes cross moving bridge sections over a shallow ravine.

### 4. Rule expansion
Wooden cover degrades after repeated hits, so waiting indefinitely gradually removes safety.

### 5. Midpoint mutation
After the checkpoint, rotating shield plates redirect shots diagonally into mechanisms.

### 6. Combination gauntlet
Shellbacks patrol the same lanes, creating a choice between stopping shells or using them to clear Sentries.

### 7. Final statement
The last bridge raises section by section as three Sentry switches are struck during continuous forward movement.

## Enemy placement purpose

- **Camp Sentry ×9:** Defines route timing and environmental interaction
- **Shellback ×3:** Cross-lane pressure and optional enemy clearing
- **Camp Critter ×3:** Prevents passive hiding behind cover

## Power-up support

- Ice Power near first destructible-cover lesson
- Size-up at checkpoint
- Optional Bubblebloom block on upper bridge

## Compass Star Coins

- **1-5-C1 — Visible mechanic challenge:** Cross the exposed upper bridge during the pause between two clearly telegraphed Sentry volleys.
- **1-5-C2 — Optional route commitment:** Rotate a shield plate to send a projectile into the lower drawbridge switch, then take the temporary low route.
- **1-5-C3 — Well hidden:** Let a Sentry destroy three apparently decorative bridge braces; the weakened section tilts into a hidden under-bridge room.

## Difficulty changes

- **Easy:** Cover survives one additional hit and diagonal reflectors show projected paths.
- **Standard:** Uses the intended layout.
- **Hard:** Sentries alternate timing and one bridge section moves continuously.
- **Nightmare:** Destroyed cover changes which reflector catches later shots, creating a persistent route-state puzzle.

**Final signature moment:** A bridge assembles in front of the player as projectile-triggered sections rise from the ravine.

**Map result:** normal exit → 1-6

# 1-6 — Glideway

**Role:** Character-mastery athletic course  
**Target time:** 2:00-2:30  
**Difficulty:** 2.2/5  
**Identity:** Use controlled aerial movement through a windy canopy while grounded lifts preserve a complete route for Hargold.

**Primary mechanic:** Mebble glide opening, steering and closing  
**Supporting mechanic:** Wind pockets and Hargold lift alternatives  
**Checkpoint:** At the giant central tree platform after the first mixed-route section, approximately 49%.

## Seven-beat course flow

### 1. Establish
A low wind pocket slows falling over solid ground, previewing glide behavior.

### 2. Safe demonstration
Mebble can glide to an upper ledge while Hargold rides a nearby lift to the same destination.

### 3. First test
A wide canopy gap requires either controlled gliding or a timed sequence of grounded lifts.

### 4. Rule expansion
Low branches require closing the glide to drop beneath them and reopening after passing.

### 5. Midpoint mutation
Wind alternates direction in visible leaf-filled pockets, changing horizontal drift.

### 6. Combination gauntlet
Acorn Bombers and moving branches force the player to choose when to sacrifice height for safety.

### 7. Final statement
A long aerial descent threads three branch gates before both hero routes converge at the goal tree.

## Enemy placement purpose

- **Acorn Bomber ×6:** Creates vertical timing hazards during glides
- **Camp Critter ×3:** Pressures Hargold lift landings
- **Spike Beetle ×3:** Blocks obvious branch tops

## Power-up support

- Size-up before first gap
- Bubblebloom near checkpoint
- Optional Fire Power on Hargold route

## Compass Star Coins

- **1-6-C1 — Visible mechanic challenge:** Glide or ride lifts through the first upper ring without touching the lower recovery branches.
- **1-6-C2 — Optional route commitment:** Close the glide early to enter a lower wind pocket, then reopen to climb its upward draft.
- **1-6-C3 — Well hidden:** Ride a moving branch beyond its apparent turnaround; the leaf trail continues behind the trunk into a rear alcove.

## Difficulty changes

- **Easy:** Wind pockets are wider and Hargold lifts wait longer.
- **Standard:** Uses the intended layout.
- **Hard:** Branch gates narrow and Bombers cover one recovery path.
- **Nightmare:** Wind pockets change after each used branch, forcing adaptation while remaining fully telegraphed by leaves.

**Final signature moment:** A descending canopy slalom where the landscape opens to reveal the entire valley below.

**Map result:** normal exit → 1-7

# 1-7 — Cliffline Fork

**Role:** Internal route-choice set piece  
**Target time:** 2:25-2:55  
**Difficulty:** 2.5/5  
**Identity:** Choose a fast exposed canopy route or a denser creek route, then face a finale that combines whichever mechanics were learned.

**Primary mechanic:** Persistent upper/lower route choice  
**Supporting mechanic:** Canopy wind versus creek logs and shells  
**Checkpoint:** At the route-rejoin camp, approximately 62%; each branch has its own checkpoint approach.

## Seven-beat course flow

### 1. Establish
A clear signpost and camera framing show both branches before commitment: canopy above, creek below.

### 2. Safe demonstration
The first obstacle on each branch previews its rule without lethal pressure: drifting branch above, floating log below.

### 3. First test
Canopy asks for timed aerial transfers; creek asks for shell control across moving logs.

### 4. Rule expansion
Canopy branches begin to fall after landing; creek logs sink under sustained weight.

### 5. Midpoint mutation
Each route crosses the other visually, allowing players to see missed rewards without switching branches.

### 6. Combination gauntlet
After rejoining, wind pushes hanging logs while Shellbacks and Acorn Bombers occupy alternating elevations.

### 7. Final statement
A collapsing cliff forces rapid transfers between high branches and low logs, summarizing both routes.

## Enemy placement purpose

- **Acorn Bomber ×5:** Canopy pressure and rejoin complexity
- **Shellback ×5:** Creek route tool and final cross-route hazard
- **Camp Sentry ×3:** Controls rejoin elevations
- **Camp Critter ×4:** Basic branch and log pressure

## Power-up support

- Route-neutral size-up before fork
- Bubblebloom on canopy branch
- Ice Power on creek branch
- Recovery block after rejoin

## Compass Star Coins

- **1-7-C1 — Visible mechanic challenge:** Canopy route: remain on the highest moving branch through its full travel cycle.
- **1-7-C2 — Optional route commitment:** Creek route: kick a Shellback across three sinking logs before the last log submerges.
- **1-7-C3 — Well hidden:** At the route crossing, follow a short coin line that stops at empty air; drop between routes onto a narrow concealed center shelf.

## Difficulty changes

- **Easy:** Both routes add recovery platforms and the final cliff collapses more slowly.
- **Standard:** Uses the intended layout.
- **Hard:** Falling branches and sinking logs recover less quickly.
- **Nightmare:** The chosen branch changes the rejoined finale: canopy completion strengthens wind; creek completion raises water and changes log height.

**Final signature moment:** The two routes physically collapse into one moving cliffside sequence.

**Map result:** normal exit → 1-9

# 1-8 — Rootbound Hollow

**Role:** Optional secret stage  
**Target time:** 2:30-3:00  
**Difficulty:** 3.2/5  
**Identity:** Navigate a dark living-root cavern where safe footing appears only through environmental clues and precise interaction.

**Primary mechanic:** Roots that shift after being stepped on  
**Supporting mechanic:** Lantern-revealed outlines and hidden footing  
**Checkpoint:** At the ancient root-heart chamber, approximately 58%.

## Seven-beat course flow

### 1. Establish
The entrance shows one root moving after a harmless step, then returning to its original position.

### 2. Safe demonstration
Lantern spores briefly outline concealed root platforms over solid ground.

### 3. First test
The player crosses a dark pit using roots whose next positions are previewed by drifting spores.

### 4. Rule expansion
Some roots rotate nearby roots when weighted, making route order matter.

### 5. Midpoint mutation
The root heart pulses on a fixed rhythm, alternately revealing platforms and waking enemies.

### 6. Combination gauntlet
Shellback weight can hold one root in place while the player crosses another; Sentry shots can illuminate spores from a distance.

### 7. Final statement
A rapid sequence asks the player to memorize three previewed root positions before the cavern goes dark.

## Enemy placement purpose

- **Shellback ×4:** Acts as movable weight and rolling hazard
- **Camp Sentry ×4:** Illuminates spores and pressures hidden footing
- **Spike Beetle ×5:** Marks unsafe root surfaces

## Power-up support

- Beanscope Lantern skill is supported but not required
- Size-up after first hidden-footing test
- Stonefist Gloves before final memory sequence

## Compass Star Coins

- **1-8-C1 — Visible mechanic challenge:** Follow the spore preview across the first full hidden-root sequence.
- **1-8-C2 — Optional route commitment:** Leave a Shellback retracted on a weight root, then cross the elevated path it holds open.
- **1-8-C3 — Expertly hidden:** Earlier, redirect a Sentry shot into a dormant spore cluster to reveal a four-symbol root sequence. Later, step on four weight roots in that order while preserving the Shellback used for the second coin. This opens a hidden chamber behind the root heart.

## Difficulty changes

- **Easy:** Spore outlines persist longer and incorrect root order resets without dropping the player.
- **Standard:** Uses the intended layout.
- **Hard:** Preview windows shorten and enemies wake one pulse earlier.
- **Nightmare:** Root positions persist after errors until the player deliberately resets them at a visible anchor.

**Final signature moment:** The cavern goes completely dark while remembered root silhouettes shift into the final bridge.

**Map result:** normal exit → 1-9

# 1-9 — Verdant Gate

**Role:** World fortress and major boss  
**Target time:** 4:30-5:30  
**Difficulty:** 3.0/5  
**Identity:** Breach a living ruin that recombines the world’s slopes, Sentry mechanisms, moving roots and wind before a five-hit Verdant Wyrm battle.

**Primary mechanic:** World-1 mechanic synthesis  
**Supporting mechanic:** Arena rotation and root destruction  
**Checkpoint:** One course checkpoint at 52%; a separate boss retry checkpoint activates at the arena door.

## Seven-beat course flow

### 1. Establish
The gate exterior uses familiar slopes and Shellbacks at higher density, signaling a mastery course rather than a tutorial.

### 2. Safe demonstration
A reinforced gate opens when a Sentry shot is redirected through a rotating root reflector.

### 3. First test
The outer ruins combine rotating stairs with wind-exposed gaps and enemy fire.

### 4. Rule expansion
Living roots repair destroyed platforms unless their glowing anchor is broken.

### 5. Midpoint mutation
Inside the gate, the route descends around a rotating stone core while poison-free root sap rises as a nonlethal timing pressure.

### 6. Combination gauntlet
A final hallway combines falling branches, Sentry switches, Shellback block chains and a short glide/lift split.

### 7. Final statement
The hallway opens directly into the circular root arena; the same reflectors and anchors become boss mechanics.

## Enemy placement purpose

- **Camp Sentry ×8:** Opens mechanisms and creates fortress pressure
- **Shellback ×5:** Breaks anchors and clears lanes
- **Spike Beetle ×5:** Restricts safe landings
- **Acorn Bomber ×4:** Adds vertical timing in exposed ruins
- **Camp Critter ×4:** Maintains ground pressure

## Power-up support

- Choice block at start
- Guaranteed size-up before midpoint
- Choice block before boss retry door

## Compass Star Coins

- **1-9-C1 — Visible mechanic challenge:** Ride the exterior rotating stair past the gate opening and jump to the visible high battlement.
- **1-9-C2 — Optional route commitment:** Destroy three repair anchors before the root bridge regrows, then use the temporary lower corridor.
- **1-9-C3 — Well hidden:** Carry a dormant Shellback through the final hallway and place it on a root weight beside the boss door; this opens a pre-arena side crypt.

## Boss — Verdant Wyrm

**Required damage events:** 5  
**Arena:** A broad circular ruin platform shown side-on. Root arms divide the platform into changing sections while the background arena rotates for presentation only.

### Damage progression

- Hit 1: bait a ground charge into a cracked root anchor, then strike the exposed head.
- Hit 2: redirect a seed projectile through a reflector to break the Wyrm’s side armor.
- Hit 3: survive an overhead root sweep and use a raised stair module to reach the exposed crest.
- Hit 4: break two healing roots while the arena is divided into three moving sections.
- Hit 5: during a continuous charge-and-seed sequence, create a Shellback ricochet or use a character-specific direct counter to shatter the final horn.

### Attack set

- Ground charge with braking tell
- Seed mortar volley
- Low root sweep
- Overhead vine rake
- Arena-dividing root growth
- Healing-root channel
- Desperate alternating charge

### Character counters

- **Hargold:** Can break damaged anchors directly and use the learned double jump to reach the crest.
- **Mebble:** Can glide over the root sweep and redirect seed shots from higher reflectors.

## Difficulty changes

- **Easy:** Fortress adds recovery platforms; boss telegraphs last longer and healing roots grow slowly.
- **Standard:** Uses the intended layout.
- **Hard:** Boss attacks chain into alternate follow-ups and arena roots remain active longer.
- **Nightmare:** Broken arena sections remain altered between phases; the Wyrm changes attack order based on the player’s position but remains deterministic.

**Final signature moment:** The Wyrm’s final defeat tears open the living gate and reveals the coastline of World 2 beyond the valley.

**Map result:** normal exit → 2-1
