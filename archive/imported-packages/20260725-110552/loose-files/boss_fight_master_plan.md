# Major Boss Fight Master Plan — Build 019

All charted bosses are retained. **Camp Head is canonically Master Mossback.** His duplicate appearance is intentional rather than an error: World 6 is the first direct confrontation, World 7 is the main-campaign finale in which his actions unleash the Fen Phantasm, and World 10 is the postgame epic rematch.

Every major boss uses five earned damage events, at least six distinct attacks, at least two arena mutations, deterministic telegraphs, and different pattern compositions by difficulty. Either hero can finish every encounter without forced repeated switching.

## World 1 — Verdant Wyrm
**World:** Verdant Vale  
**Role:** Vale Dragon  
**Fight identity:** living-arena duel

### Room — Root-Crown Basin
A broad circular terraced basin on a strict side-scrolling plane, with three root shelves, a shallow center hollow and a breakable canopy overhead.

**Presentation layers:**
- rear waterfall and distant vale
- playable root terraces
- foreground vines that retract during attacks

**Arena mutations:**
- After damage 2, roots split the center floor into two elevations.
- After damage 4, the canopy tears open and wind changes seed and spore trajectories.

**Instant-death arena hazards:**
- deep root chasm exposed only in final phase

### Unique attacks
1. **Root Rake** — Tell: Front claws dig in and roots pulse toward the active lane. Counterplay: Wall-jump or change terrace before the roots erupt.
2. **Seed Mortar** — Tell: Back spines glow in a three-beat sequence. Counterplay: Read the arcs; seeds become temporary bounce pods after impact.
3. **Canopy Dive** — Tell: Wyrm coils upward and leaf shadows cross the floor. Counterplay: Move beneath the safe shadow gap, then punish the landing recovery.
4. **Tail Scythe** — Tell: Tail leaves drag and the rear terrace bends. Counterplay: Jump, duck-slide or use a root shelf depending on height.
5. **Spore Breath** — Tell: Throat sacs inflate green, then yellow. Counterplay: Use moving air pockets; spores linger as denial clouds.
6. **Root Cage** — Tell: Four seed marks appear around the player. Counterplay: Break one weak root or escape vertically before closure.
7. **Burrow Surge** — Tell: Soil ridge tracks the player for one second. Counterplay: Reverse direction late; the missed surge exposes a scale.
8. **Thorn Halo** — Tell: Body curls and thorns point outward. Counterplay: Bounce from a seed pod over the expanding ring.

### Five earned damage events
1. Bait Burrow Surge into the cracked center root, exposing the jaw crest.
2. Use a Seed Mortar pod to reach and strike the left shoulder scale.
3. Trigger both terrace sap vents so Spore Breath backfires and exposes the chest.
4. Survive Root Cage, then attack the exposed tail knot before it regrows.
5. During the canopy-open storm, ride a pod above Thorn Halo and ground-slam the crown plate.

### Hero advantages
- **Hargold:** Breaks a cage root faster and produces a wider final crown-slam window.
- **Mebble:** Reaches shoulder and canopy openings with fewer pod bounces.

### Difficulty composition
- **Easy:** Longer soil and seed tells; one cage root is pre-cracked.
- **Standard:** Base pattern.
- **Hard:** Root Rake can branch after Seed Mortar; fewer permanent terraces.
- **Nightmare:** Burrow ridges fake once, spore clouds persist across the next attack, and arena mutations occur one event earlier.

## World 2 — Wraithbound
**World:** Drowned Coast  
**Role:** Specter of the Drowned Coast  
**Fight identity:** visibility and tide-control duel

### Room — The Bell-Tide Chapel
A ruined coastal chapel with a central nave, two raised choir balconies and a water channel beneath grated floor sections.

**Presentation layers:**
- storm windows and drowned statues
- main nave and balconies
- foreground bell ropes used as readable timing markers

**Arena mutations:**
- After damage 2, the tide floods the lower nave and converts grates into swimming passages.
- After damage 4, the roof collapses, moonlight creates only three safe visibility cones.

**Instant-death arena hazards:**
- deep undertow at the far edges during high tide

### Unique attacks
1. **Spectral Scythe** — Tell: One hand condenses into a crescent and the nearest candles lean away. Counterplay: Jump the low cut or wall-jump over the returning high cut.
2. **Drowned Procession** — Tell: Pews frost over in sequence. Counterplay: Use balcony gaps while spectral hands march through the nave.
3. **Black-Tide Pulse** — Tell: Bell tolls once and water glows beneath the floor. Counterplay: Reach a grate or balcony before the surge.
4. **Choir of Doubles** — Tell: Four silhouettes appear but only one casts ripples. Counterplay: Attack or ring a bell at the ripple-producing form.
5. **Lantern Possession** — Tell: A wall lantern burns violet. Counterplay: Knock the possessed lantern beam into Wraithbound.
6. **Undertow Hands** — Tell: Water circles tighten around the player. Counterplay: Swim or run across current, then use the exposed hand as a platform.
7. **Bell-Silence Field** — Tell: All sound cuts out and ropes freeze. Counterplay: Read window lightning instead of audio until the field ends.
8. **Phantom Crossing** — Tell: Wraithbound marks three portal arches. Counterplay: Stand outside the matching moonlit arch and punish the failed dash.

### Five earned damage events
1. Ring the left bell while Wraithbound crosses its reflection line, making the first body solid.
2. Redirect a possessed lantern beam through the choir mirror into the true double.
3. Ride an Undertow Hand to the balcony and break the spectral anchor above the nave.
4. Open both flood grates during Black-Tide Pulse so the rebound current tears away the robe armor.
5. In the moonlit final phase, identify the ripple-bearing form and strike it before the third silent toll.

### Hero advantages
- **Hargold:** Moves possessed lantern mounts with fewer hits and resists ordinary current push.
- **Mebble:** Reaches balconies and crosses flooded gaps more quickly.

### Difficulty composition
- **Easy:** True doubles retain a faint ripple trail and undertow pull is weaker.
- **Standard:** Base pattern.
- **Hard:** Scythe returns at a different height and the tide remains high longer.
- **Nightmare:** Bell-Silence overlaps Phantom Crossing and false doubles can create harmless decoy ripples.

## World 3 — Luminite Golem
**World:** Luminite Glade  
**Role:** Sentinel of the Glade  
**Fight identity:** resonance and destructible-armor fight

### Room — Prismheart Quarry
A long crystal quarry chamber with three resonance pillars, a central mining lift and faceted walls that redirect beams.

**Presentation layers:**
- deep crystal seam
- playable quarry deck and lift
- foreground crystal fins that show resonance direction

**Arena mutations:**
- After damage 2, the center lift collapses into two moving slabs.
- After damage 4, the back wall fractures and creates ricochet angles for the final beam cycle.

**Instant-death arena hazards:**
- bottomless crystal shaft below collapsed lift sections

### Unique attacks
1. **Granite Hammer** — Tell: Right arm enlarges and dust falls from the ceiling. Counterplay: Move behind the marked impact zone; the strike charges a pillar.
2. **Shard Rain** — Tell: Shoulder crystals brighten from left to right. Counterplay: Shelter behind uncharged pillars or keep moving through the gaps.
3. **Prism Beam** — Tell: Chest aperture opens and wall facets illuminate. Counterplay: Rotate a pillar or use safe shadow wedges.
4. **Resonance Quake** — Tell: Both fists touch the floor and pillars hum. Counterplay: Jump each expanding floor wave; wall jump avoids the final double wave.
5. **Crystal Cage** — Tell: Six floor points sparkle around the player. Counterplay: Exit before growth or break the lowest seam after closure.
6. **Rolling Core** — Tell: Limbs retract and the chest rotates. Counterplay: Use lift slabs to avoid the pass; redirect it into a pillar.
7. **Prism Copies** — Tell: Wall facets show three chest cores. Counterplay: Read dust and floor weight to identify the physical body.
8. **Seam Eruption** — Tell: Cracks beneath the player glow cyan. Counterplay: Keep moving, then use the raised seam as temporary high ground.

### Five earned damage events
1. Bait Granite Hammer into the left resonance pillar, then strike the exposed wrist seam.
2. Redirect Rolling Core into the right pillar to break the opposite knee armor.
3. Align two charged pillars so Prism Beam rebounds into the chest aperture.
4. Use the separated lift slabs to reach and disable both shoulder crystals during Shard Rain.
5. In the fractured chamber, complete a three-surface beam ricochet and ground-slam the exposed Prismheart.

### Hero advantages
- **Hargold:** Breaks cage seams and exposed stone joints faster.
- **Mebble:** Can move between lift slabs and shoulder crystals with less setup.

### Difficulty composition
- **Easy:** Pillars remain charged longer and ricochet guides stay visible.
- **Standard:** Base pattern.
- **Hard:** Quake waves overlap Shard Rain and pillar charge decays.
- **Nightmare:** One wall facet changes angle after each beam and Rolling Core can reverse once.

## World 4 — Altitude Archmage
**World:** Skyreach Range  
**Role:** Keeper of the Wind  
**Fight identity:** air-current and spell-redirection duel

### Room — Stormglass Observatory
Three floating observation decks orbit a central lightning rod while remaining on a strict side-scrolling path; bridges extend and retract between them.

**Presentation layers:**
- cloud sea and distant peaks
- three playable decks and rod
- foreground astrolabe rings showing wind direction

**Arena mutations:**
- After damage 2, one bridge breaks and decks begin changing height.
- After damage 4, the dome opens and direct lightning periodically charges the rod.

**Instant-death arena hazards:**
- falling below the cloud decks

### Unique attacks
1. **Feather Lance** — Tell: Staff feathers align into a straight line. Counterplay: Deflect with a shutter or guide it into a grounding node.
2. **Gust Wall** — Tell: Astrolabe rings rotate toward one side. Counterplay: Run, crouch-slide behind cover or use wall surfaces.
3. **Lightning Script** — Tell: Runes write themselves above targeted floor tiles. Counterplay: Leave marked tiles; bolts charge nearby nodes.
4. **Cloud Double** — Tell: Three copies appear, but debris bends only around the real one. Counterplay: Strike the copy affecting wind particles.
5. **Reverse-Gravity Pulse** — Tell: The central rod flashes white twice. Counterplay: Use the temporary ceiling ledges, then land as gravity restores.
6. **Cyclone Cage** — Tell: Four wind knots form around the active deck. Counterplay: Ride the inner updraft and exit through the rotating gap.
7. **Teleport Staff Drop** — Tell: A circular shadow tracks the player. Counterplay: Late dodge; the embedded staff becomes a climb point.
8. **Storm Quill Barrage** — Tell: Robe hem fans outward. Counterplay: Move through the staggered lane gaps instead of outrunning the volley.

### Five earned damage events
1. Ground Feather Lance through the left shutter node, stunning the Archmage on the first deck.
2. Use the embedded staff as a climb point and strike the first robe clasp.
3. Identify the true Cloud Double and redirect its own Lightning Script into the center rod.
4. During reverse gravity, cross the ceiling ledges and disable the second clasp.
5. Survive the open-dome cyclone, then use the fully charged rod to ground the final spell shield.

### Hero advantages
- **Hargold:** Maintains ground position against gusts and opens jammed shutters quickly.
- **Mebble:** Uses glide and higher jump to recover between moving decks.

### Difficulty composition
- **Easy:** Deck height changes are slower and true-copy debris is brighter.
- **Standard:** Base pattern.
- **Hard:** Gust Wall chains into Quill Barrage and bridges retract sooner.
- **Nightmare:** Gravity pulses can interrupt deck transfers and lightning nodes retain hazardous charge.

## World 5 — Sand Wraith
**World:** Scorchglass Dunes  
**Role:** Spirit of the Dunes  
**Fight identity:** shifting-floor and mirage hunt

### Room — The Buried Sun Court
A half-buried solar temple with three sand basins, two stone arches and a central hourglass dais.

**Presentation layers:**
- dune storm and buried statues
- sand basins, arches and dais
- foreground chains showing wind pull

**Arena mutations:**
- After damage 2, the temple floor tilts and sand flows continuously between basins.
- After damage 4, heat turns exposed sand paths into temporary glass platforms.

**Instant-death arena hazards:**
- deep quicksand wells at basin drains

### Unique attacks
1. **Chain Scythe** — Tell: The chain lifts from the sand before the blade appears. Counterplay: Jump the first sweep and duck-slide the returning high sweep.
2. **Dune Breaker** — Tell: A straight ridge races beneath the sand. Counterplay: Change elevation or wall-jump from an arch.
3. **Mirage Court** — Tell: Four Wraiths appear; only one disturbs hanging chains. Counterplay: Read chain movement and attack the physical form.
4. **Glass Shard Cyclone** — Tell: Sun mirrors focus on one basin. Counterplay: Move behind arches or use the calm center of the cyclone.
5. **Burial Hands** — Tell: Finger-shaped depressions form around the player. Counterplay: Keep moving; missed hands become climbable for a moment.
6. **Hourglass Drain** — Tell: The dais rotates and all sand begins flowing inward. Counterplay: Climb arches and stop the drain valves.
7. **Sand Serpent** — Tell: A long curved wake circles the room. Counterplay: Bait the serpent into a mirror pillar.
8. **Sunblind Flash** — Tell: Mirrors open in sequence. Counterplay: Face away or stand in an arch shadow; blinded controls are never inverted.

### Five earned damage events
1. Bait Sand Serpent into the left mirror pillar, revealing the first chain anchor.
2. Use Burial Hands as steps to reach and break the upper hourglass seal.
3. Identify the physical Mirage by chain movement and strike before the copies recombine.
4. Close both drain valves during Hourglass Drain so the rebound exposes the core chain.
5. Cross the newly glassed sand during the final cyclone and ground-slam the hourglass dais.

### Hero advantages
- **Hargold:** Breaks chain anchors and resists inward sand drag.
- **Mebble:** Uses Burial Hands and glass paths to reach seals more directly.

### Difficulty composition
- **Easy:** Quicksand develops slowly and mirage chain clues are pronounced.
- **Standard:** Base pattern.
- **Hard:** Sunblind Flash overlaps the serpent wake and glass platforms expire sooner.
- **Nightmare:** False ridges appear during Dune Breaker and the sand-flow direction changes after each event.

## World 6 — Master Mossback
**World:** Camp Dominion  
**Role:** Warlord of Camp — First Confrontation  
**Fight identity:** weapon-sabotage command duel

### Room — The Grand Mess Hall Arsenal
A fortified timber-and-iron command hall with a central mess table, two supply balconies, a turkey-cannon rail and retractable floor hatches.

**Presentation layers:**
- camp banners and marching silhouettes
- main hall, balconies and cannon rail
- foreground hanging cookware that reacts to shockwaves

**Arena mutations:**
- After damage 2, the mess table splits into moving cover sections.
- After damage 4, Mossback tears down the rear banner and reveals the bass-slap stage and escape lift.

**Instant-death arena hazards:**
- heavy crusher hatch activated only by a clearly telegraphed command lever

### Unique attacks
1. **Turkey Cannon Salvo** — Tell: Cannon gobbles and feather pressure builds at the muzzle. Counterplay: Use cover; stunned turkey shots can be redirected into machinery.
2. **Acorn Mortar Grid** — Tell: Mossback points to floor sectors in a fixed rhythm. Counterplay: Move through the unmarked sector and use craters as temporary cover.
3. **Command Whistle** — Tell: He raises a brass whistle and nearby doors flash. Counterplay: Interrupt a door lever or use summoned mobs against equipment.
4. **Chain-Hook Reel** — Tell: Hook spins twice before launch. Counterplay: Jump, duck or let it pull a supply crate into a switch.
5. **Supply-Cart Charge** — Tell: Rail lights illuminate from the far wall. Counterplay: Change elevation; the cart can smash armor panels.
6. **Oil-Flare Toss** — Tell: He shakes a lantern and the wick turns blue. Counterplay: Knock the oil jar aside before the flare lands.
7. **Bass Slap Shockwave** — Tell: Low-health phase; Mossback plants his feet and raises the fish. Counterplay: Jump staggered waves and punish the long follow-through.
8. **Cannon Recoil Rush** — Tell: He overpacks the turkey cannon and braces backward. Counterplay: Clear the recoil lane; the backward launch exposes him.

### Five earned damage events
1. Redirect a stunned turkey projectile into the left cannon support, dropping Mossback into reach.
2. Use Chain-Hook Reel to drag a supply crate onto the balcony release switch.
3. Bait Supply-Cart Charge through the right armor panel and strike during the crash.
4. Turn Command Whistle summons against the oil pump, forcing Mossback into the open.
5. Survive the bass-slap sequence, then use cannon recoil to expose the command crest for the fifth hit.

### Hero advantages
- **Hargold:** Breaks supply crates and braces against hook pull.
- **Mebble:** Reaches balcony releases without waiting for the moving table.

### Difficulty composition
- **Easy:** Fewer simultaneous summons and longer cannon recoil.
- **Standard:** Base pattern.
- **Hard:** Whistle summons overlap mortar sectors and table cover moves faster.
- **Nightmare:** Mossback can cancel one command into Chain-Hook Reel and bass waves reflect from the hall walls.

## World 7 — Fen Phantasm
**World:** Toxic Fen  
**Role:** Illusor of the Deep — Main Campaign Finale  
**Fight identity:** multi-act pursuit and seal-breaking finale

### Room — The Sunken Throne and Deep Fen Pursuit
Begins in a circular throne rotunda, breaks into a long rightward pursuit across collapsing fen machinery, and ends on three anchored platforms above the abyssal pool.

**Presentation layers:**
- endless toxic fen and giant shadow form
- strict side-scrolling rotunda, pursuit route and final platforms
- foreground reeds and reflections used to reveal illusions

**Arena mutations:**
- After seal 2, the rotunda collapses into the pursuit route.
- After seal 4, the final platforms detach and orbit the exposed heart while remaining on one gameplay plane.

**Instant-death arena hazards:**
- poison abyss and crushing throne machinery; both cause immediate life loss

### Unique attacks
1. **Mirror Maw** — Tell: Reflections open mouths before the real surface breaks. Counterplay: Use reflection timing to avoid the true bite lane.
2. **Poison Tide** — Tell: The distant fen lowers before a wall of poison rises. Counterplay: Climb or use temporary wreckage; poison is instant death.
3. **Shadow Tendrils** — Tell: Black roots mark three floor points. Counterplay: Move through the delayed gaps; cut tendrils become temporary rails.
4. **False Goal** — Tell: A glowing exit appears without a matching reflection. Counterplay: Ignore it; approaching triggers a punish wave.
5. **Illusion Stampede** — Tell: Camp silhouettes run across the background before entering play. Counterplay: Read which silhouettes cast foreground shadows.
6. **Abyss Beam** — Tell: The heart opens and sweeps a thin targeting line. Counterplay: Use moving cover or cross through the beam during its brief split.
7. **Platform Possession** — Tell: One final platform grows violet veins. Counterplay: Leave it before it flips, then use its underside latch when it returns.
8. **Deep Pull** — Tell: Water spirals toward the center heart. Counterplay: Run outward, wall-jump anchors or use detached tendril rails.
9. **Phantom Collapse** — Tell: The giant outline raises both hands over the pursuit route. Counterplay: Keep pace while its strikes open and close the path.

### Five earned damage events
1. In the throne rotunda, reveal the true heart reflection and break the first fen seal.
2. Redirect Abyss Beam through two mirror reeds to rupture the second seal and trigger the pursuit.
3. During the pursuit, bait Phantom Collapse into the chained gate, exposing the third seal without stopping movement.
4. At the final platforms, use a possessed platform underside latch to tear open the fourth seal.
5. Survive Deep Pull and the final Poison Tide, then strike the exposed heart after all three anchors synchronize.

### Hero advantages
- **Hargold:** Breaks seal housings quickly and holds position against Deep Pull.
- **Mebble:** Recovers between collapsing pursuit platforms and reaches underside latches more easily.

### Difficulty composition
- **Easy:** Poison tide rises slower and false goals flicker visibly.
- **Standard:** Base pattern.
- **Hard:** Tendrils remain active during Abyss Beam and pursuit gaps narrow.
- **Nightmare:** Reflection clues are shorter, possessed platforms change order, and the final tide begins before anchor synchronization finishes.

## World 8 — Bone Crusher
**World:** Pit of Echoes  
**Role:** Tyrant of the Pit  
**Fight identity:** colossal joint-breaking and echo-resonance fight

### Room — Ossuary Resonance Pit
A deep amphitheater of rib bridges, hanging bone chains and three echo drums built into the walls.

**Presentation layers:**
- endless ossuary depth
- rib bridges and drum platforms
- foreground chains telegraphing impact direction

**Arena mutations:**
- After damage 2, Bone Crusher tears down the center rib bridge and uses it as a club.
- After damage 4, the floor gives way to hanging chain platforms over the pit.

**Instant-death arena hazards:**
- bottomless ossuary pit and heavy bone crush

### Unique attacks
1. **Chain Flail** — Tell: Shoulder sockets rotate and chains tighten. Counterplay: Use chain shadows to read the sweep and jump between ribs.
2. **Echo Stomp** — Tell: One foot rises while wall drums pulse. Counterplay: Jump the visible wave; reflected echo follows from one wall.
3. **Rib Cage Prison** — Tell: Loose ribs point inward around the player. Counterplay: Exit early or break the marked weak rib.
4. **Skull Roll** — Tell: Chest cavity opens and a giant skull drops. Counterplay: Use slopes to redirect the skull into a joint.
5. **Bone Rain** — Tell: Ceiling dust falls in safe and unsafe columns. Counterplay: Read the dust gaps instead of reacting to falling bones.
6. **Detached Arm Hunt** — Tell: One shoulder unlocks and the arm crawls independently. Counterplay: Use the hand as a moving platform after its slam.
7. **Grave Breath** — Tell: Jaw runes light from back to front. Counterplay: Hide behind thick ribs or climb above the breath line.
8. **Pit Drag** — Tell: Lower chains rattle toward the center. Counterplay: Break or evade the grabbing hooks before the pull.

### Five earned damage events
1. Redirect Skull Roll into the left ankle joint.
2. Strike the exposed shoulder after Detached Arm Hunt misses its grab.
3. Beat both wall drums during Echo Stomp so the resonance cracks the rib armor.
4. Use the torn rib bridge as a lever to break the spine chain.
5. On the hanging-platform phase, synchronize all three echo drums and ground-slam the exposed skull crest.

### Hero advantages
- **Hargold:** Breaks prison ribs and resists chain pull.
- **Mebble:** Navigates hanging chains and detached-hand platforms more safely.

### Difficulty composition
- **Easy:** Drum windows are longer and chain shadows are brighter.
- **Standard:** Base pattern.
- **Hard:** Echo waves reflect twice and Bone Rain overlaps the detached arm.
- **Nightmare:** One drum changes pitch after each event and the final chain platforms swing asynchronously.

## World 9 — Temps Warden
**World:** Chrono Archive  
**Role:** Guardian of Time Itself  
**Fight identity:** deterministic replay and timeline-control duel

### Room — The Three-Hour Archive
A clockwork library divided into Past, Present and Future decks connected by a central pendulum lift; all remain on one side-scrolling plane.

**Presentation layers:**
- endless clock stacks
- three timeline decks and pendulum
- foreground clock hands showing upcoming replay direction

**Arena mutations:**
- After damage 2, the Warden desynchronizes the decks so their platforms age at different rates.
- After damage 4, all decks overlap into one unstable composite timeline.

**Instant-death arena hazards:**
- timeline voids where aged platforms disappear

### Unique attacks
1. **Time Slice** — Tell: A clock hand traces the exact attack line one second early. Counterplay: Leave the line before the delayed cut.
2. **Replay Echo** — Tell: A translucent record of the player repeats the last three seconds. Counterplay: Plan movement so the echo does not intersect the current route.
3. **Rewind Mine** — Tell: A gear mine records its placed position and flashes backward. Counterplay: Trigger it from a safe location; it returns along its original path.
4. **Age Platform** — Tell: Books and metal rust from one side. Counterplay: Cross before collapse or move to a younger timeline deck.
5. **Clock-Hand Sweep** — Tell: The central clock chimes the sweep height. Counterplay: Jump low sweep, duck high sweep, wall-jump the split sweep.
6. **Stasis Field** — Tell: Blue numbers stop counting around one area. Counterplay: Avoid entry or use a moving object to hold a switch inside it.
7. **Future Duplicate** — Tell: A gold image shows where the Warden will appear. Counterplay: Prepare the correct counter before materialization.
8. **Time Debt** — Tell: Collected time sparks orbit the player. Counterplay: Deposit sparks at a clock socket before the countdown expires.

### Five earned damage events
1. Use a Rewind Mine to strike the Warden at its recorded past position.
2. Arrange Replay Echo to activate two timeline switches simultaneously.
3. Cross to the Future deck and interrupt the predicted Duplicate materialization.
4. Deposit three Time Debt sparks into the pendulum socket, freezing the Warden instead of the player.
5. In the composite timeline, survive the recorded three-second replay and strike the core at the exact shared chime.

### Hero advantages
- **Hargold:** Carries heavy clock weights through Stasis Fields.
- **Mebble:** Transfers between timeline decks with less dependence on the pendulum.

### Difficulty composition
- **Easy:** Replay paths remain visible and age timers are longer.
- **Standard:** Base pattern.
- **Hard:** The Warden records four seconds and Time Slice overlaps platform aging.
- **Nightmare:** One replay is mirrored, future duplicates can cancel into Clock-Hand Sweep, and the final shared chime window is shorter.

## World 10 — Master Mossback
**World:** The Last Camp  
**Role:** Epic Final Boss — Last Expedition  
**Fight identity:** three-room command-machine rematch

### Room — Mossback Command Citadel
A three-stage encounter: command rotunda, moving Ironwood Dreadnought deck, and a ruined summit around a stolen Chronus engine.

**Presentation layers:**
- army and storm backdrop
- strict side-scrolling rooms linked by short transitions
- foreground banners, chains and clockwork tells

**Arena mutations:**
- After damage 2, the rotunda wall breaks and the battle continues on the moving Dreadnought.
- After damage 4, the Dreadnought crashes into the summit and the Chronus engine rewinds arena hazards.

**Instant-death arena hazards:**
- dreadnought crushers, abyss falls and Chronus engine implosion zones

### Unique attacks
1. **Grand Turkey Salvo** — Tell: Three cannon chambers gobble in a visible rhythm. Counterplay: Use deck shutters and return selected shots into weapon ports.
2. **Sentry Command Grid** — Tell: Mossback draws firing lanes on a command map. Counterplay: Move through lane gaps or reflect shots into exposed controls.
3. **Ironwood Ram** — Tell: The dreadnought prow lowers and track gears accelerate. Counterplay: Climb deck fixtures; the ram can break shield plating.
4. **Grapple Muster** — Tell: Multiple hooks spin at different speeds. Counterplay: Read spin count for launch order and use hooks to move crates.
5. **Bomb-Bird Wing** — Tell: Whistle pattern calls a formation overhead. Counterplay: Break formation or bait bombs into deck engines.
6. **Royal Bassquake** — Tell: Mossback raises the giant bass and engine gauges spike. Counterplay: Traverse alternating floor and air shockwaves.
7. **Chronus Rewind** — Tell: The engine records the arena in gold outlines. Counterplay: Remember prior hazard positions; they replay in reverse.
8. **False Surrender** — Tell: Mossback drops the cannon but keeps one hand on the command cord. Counterplay: Do not approach until the cord slackens; interrupt the trap lever.
9. **Last Expedition Charge** — Tell: He discards all weapons, lowers his shoulder and the summit cracks. Counterplay: Use the remaining anchor points to redirect the charge into the Chronus brace.

### Five earned damage events
1. Return a turkey salvo through the correct rotunda port to destroy the first command shield.
2. Use Grapple Muster to swing a supply crate into the exposed map console.
3. On the Dreadnought, bait Ironwood Ram through two engine braces and strike during the derail recovery.
4. Turn Bomb-Bird Wing against the Chronus engine housing after the summit crash.
5. Survive Chronus Rewind and Royal Bassquake together, then redirect Last Expedition Charge into the final brace and deliver the last hit.

### Hero advantages
- **Hargold:** Moves heavy supply crates and holds against deck acceleration.
- **Mebble:** Traverses dreadnought fixtures and rewind gaps more efficiently.

### Difficulty composition
- **Easy:** Command lanes remain marked longer and one rewind hazard is omitted.
- **Standard:** Base pattern.
- **Hard:** Dreadnought movement persists during volleys and Mossback chains commands.
- **Nightmare:** Chronus Rewind replays two prior arena states, False Surrender can occur after event 3 or 4, and the final charge route has fewer anchors.
